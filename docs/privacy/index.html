Privacy Policy — Undercurrent Labs Ad-Free Games

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what the games in Undercurrent Labs Ad-Free Games (such as Ad Free Sudoku and Ad Free Twos, together "the games", each one "the app") do with your information. It covers every game in the suite. The games are published by Undercurrent Labs ("we", "us"), based in Bengaluru, India.

In short: no game shows advertisements of any kind, in either the free or the Pro version. Each game works entirely on your device. We operate no server, there is no account system, and your name, scores and game progress never leave your phone. The only information that goes anywhere is handled by Google if you choose to buy the Pro unlock.

1. Information the app stores on your device

Each game saves the following in your device's local browser storage. This information stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and we cannot see it:

  • Player name — the pseudonym you choose on first launch (for example "Blue Tokai"). This is a nickname, not your real name, and you are free to invent anything.
  • Game statistics — depending on the game: scores, times, moves, mistakes, win streaks and your rating.
  • Saved game — your game in progress, so you can close the app and return to it.
  • Settings — chosen theme, and preferences that belong to a particular game (for example lightning mode and hint balance in Sudoku).
  • A randomly generated device identifier (some games, such as Ad Free Sudoku) — created on your device to distinguish your own records. It is not linked to you, your phone number, or any Google account.
  • Purchase status — whether the Pro unlock has been activated.

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. You can also clear your scores and saved game at any time from within each game: tap the version number in the top bar, then "Reset scores & start clean".

2. Information we collect

None. We operate no servers, run no analytics, and have no way to identify you or contact you unless you choose to email us.

3. Advertising

There is none. The games contain no advertisements, no advertising SDK, and no ad network code — in the free version or the Pro version. Nothing in any game reads your advertising identifier or builds a profile of you.

4. Purchases

The Pro unlock is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or handle your card details, bank information, or billing address. Google provides us only with anonymised sales reports. Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policy.

5. Sharing your results

The games include buttons that let you share your score or rank. These open WhatsApp (or your device's share menu) with a message pre-written for you. Nothing is sent until you choose a recipient and send it yourself, and the message contains only your chosen player name and your game statistics.

7. Children

The games are general-audience puzzle and arcade games and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, and show no advertising, there is nothing collected from children for us or anyone else to delete.

8. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or erase. For data collected by Google through Google Play Billing when you make a purchase, please exercise your rights through Google at myaccount.google.com.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the games' update notes.
